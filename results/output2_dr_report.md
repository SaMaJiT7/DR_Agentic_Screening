## Other treatments your eye doctor may discuss

Your eye doctor may also discuss these, depending on what they find:
- Focal or grid laser (for macular oedema): Targeted laser to leaking blood vessels away from the centre of the macula, used for localised swelling rather than the whole retina.
- Intravitreal corticosteroid (for macular oedema that does not settle): A slow-release steroid implant or injection into the eye to reduce swelling. Your eye doctor will discuss the risks, including raised eye pressure and cataract.
- Vitrectomy (for vitreous haemorrhage or retinal detachment): Surgery to remove blood or scar tissue from inside the eye when bleeding does not clear or the retina is being pulled away.
- Control of glucose, blood pressure and cholesterol (for slowing progression at every grade): Keeping HbA1c, blood pressure and cholesterol in target supports every other treatment and lowers the chance of progression.
